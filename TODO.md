# Grammar & Clarity Review

Check grammar and clarity for each blog post. Every post needs the pass — one
checked box per post. While reading, watch for: run-on or missing punctuation,
duplicated or leading `#` headings that repeat `title:`, scraped junk left in
`description` (`!!! note`, `ORIGINAL POST BELOW:`, `Here''s a breakdown:`),
misleading or missing article/a-an agreement, tense drift, and command/prose
mismatches.
